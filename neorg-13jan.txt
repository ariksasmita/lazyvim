  The Issue:

  When you switch workspaces, Neovim does cd to that workspace directory. So:

  Before:
  notevault/

  └── assets/

  notevault/

  │   └── project.md
  └── assets/
      └── image.png
  In project.md: ![image](assets/image.png) ❌ Broken! (looking in work/assets/)

  Solutions:

  Option 1: Keep Assets Local (Recommended)

  Each workspace has its own assets:
  notevault/
  ├── work/
  │   ├── project.md
  │   └── assets/
  │       └── image.png
  ├── personal/
  │   ├── journal.md
  │   └── assets/
  │       └── photo.png

  Option 2: Symlink Shared Assets

  cd ~/notevault/work
  ln -s ../assets assets

  Option 3: Use Root-Level References

  Update all asset links to use absolute paths from root:
  ![image](/Users/sasmitai/.../notevault/assets/image.png)
  (Not portable, but works)

  Option 4: Don't Workspace Notes with Assets

  Keep notes with heavy asset dependencies in the "default" workspace (root level), only workspace notes without many assets.

  My Recommendation:

  Start with Option 1 - keep assets local to each workspace. It's:
  - ✅ Portable (works if you move folders)
  - ✅ Self-contained
  - ✅ Works with Telescope find/grep
  - ✅ No symlinks needed
